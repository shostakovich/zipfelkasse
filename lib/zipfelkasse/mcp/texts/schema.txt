Database of Zipfelkasse (SQLite). A single group.
Conventions: amounts are INTEGER in euro cents (divide by 100.0 for euros). Calendar dates are TEXT 'YYYY-MM-DD', timestamps (inserted_at, updated_at, *_at) TEXT RFC 3339 in UTC. Booleans are 0/1.
Data values (names, titles, categories, notes) are stored as entered, often in German.
Case-insensitive search: title_folded and notes_folded are case-folded copies of title and notes (Unicode-correct, "ß" becomes "ss"). Fold the search term the same way and compare with them, e.g. instr(e.title_folded, 'bäckerei') > 0; lower() and LIKE only ignore the case of ASCII letters.

Tables:
- participants: people in the group. archived_at set = archived (no longer active, their entries remain).
- categories: categories (name, position = display order, archived_at).
- expenses: expenses AND reimbursements.
  * deleted_at set = deleted (soft delete) → ALWAYS filter with "deleted_at IS NULL".
  * amount_cents: amount in euro cents (converted for foreign currency). paid_by_id: who paid (participants.id). category_id NULL = no category.
  * reimbursement = 1: reimbursement – paid_by_id paid money to the person in the single expense_shares row. It is not an expense
    (expense analyses use "reimbursement = 0"), but it counts for balances.
  * split_mode: equal (evenly), shares (by shares), percent (by percentage), amount (fixed amounts).
  * Foreign currency: original_currency ('EUR' if none), original_amount (amount in the smallest unit of that currency),
    fx_rate (units of foreign currency per 1 EUR, ECB format), fx_source ('ecb' = ECB reference rate, 'manual' = entered manually, NULL for EUR). amount_cents is already converted.
  * title_folded, notes_folded: case-folded copies of title and notes (see above).
  * recurring_expense_id: created automatically from a recurring rule (recurring_expenses.id). inserted_at/updated_at: timestamps.
- expense_shares: split of each expense across people. amount_cents = this person's share in cents (sum per expense = expenses.amount_cents).
  weight depends on split_mode: equal 1, shares the share count, percent basis points (sum 10000), amount the amount in the smallest unit of original_currency (sum = original_amount; cents for EUR).
- recurring_expenses: rules for recurring expenses (frequency weekly|monthly|yearly, start_date = anchor of all occurrences, next_date, active, created_by_id).
  template = the expense as JSON without date: title, category_id, paid_by_id, notes, reimbursement, split_mode, amount_cents, original_amount,
  original_currency, fx_rate, fx_source, parts [{participant_id, weight}].
- activities: change log (inserted_at, actor_id NULL = system, action expense_created|expense_updated|expense_deleted|settings_updated|recurring_created|recurring_deleted,
  expense_id, details = JSON with only the keys that apply: title, amount_cents, changes [{field, old, new}] and text, in German as shown in the app).
- fx_rates: exchange rates per currency, source ('ecb' or 'manual') and date (foreign currency per 1 EUR).
  A day can have both an ECB and a manual rate; the most recent manual rate on or before a date takes precedence over the ECB rate.
- settings: the settings of the group (a single row, group_name).
YNAB tables (credentials) are not visible via MCP.

Balance of a person = sum of amount_cents of the expenses they paid (paid_by_id) − sum of their shares in expense_shares
(only deleted_at IS NULL, reimbursements included). Positive = is owed money.

Example – Anna's share per category in 2026:
SELECT coalesce(c.name, 'No category') AS category, sum(x.amount_cents) / 100.0 AS euros
FROM expenses e
JOIN expense_shares x ON x.expense_id = e.id
JOIN participants p ON p.id = x.participant_id AND p.name = 'Anna'
LEFT JOIN categories c ON c.id = e.category_id
WHERE e.deleted_at IS NULL AND e.reimbursement = 0 AND e.date BETWEEN '2026-01-01' AND '2026-12-31'
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC;
